# Recommendation contract

Every material recommendation from any skill in this repo uses this shape. If a field can't be filled honestly, write `unknown` rather than guessing.

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **What changed** | The signal or event, with source and date |
| **Why it matters** | The business pressure or deal consequence it creates |
| **Evidence** | Links, quotes from notes, dates. Minimum one per claim |
| **Confidence** | High / Medium / Low, with one line on why |
| **Recommended action** | One concrete thing to do, with a timeframe |
| **Expected outcome** | What success looks like if the action works |
| **Don't** | Optional. The tempting move that would hurt (e.g. "don't send revised pricing yet") |
| **Needs seller's call** | What the skill can't judge: relationship history, internal politics, timing feel |
| **Seller approval required** | Yes for anything customer-facing, priced or sent |

## Confidence guide

- **High:** two or more independent, recent, cited signals point the same way, and internal data confirms.
- **Medium:** one strong signal, or several weak ones, with gaps in internal data.
- **Low:** inference from a single or older signal. Worth a look, not a bet.

## Example

> **What changed:** Group announced a KL shared-services centre, ~300 roles over 12 months (The Edge, 3 Oct).
> **Why it matters:** Recruiter capacity and time-to-fill pressure in a new market.
> **Evidence:** [link], annual report hiring guidance [link].
> **Confidence:** Medium — one strong signal; no internal data on current recruiter headcount.
> **Recommended action:** Book a 30-min hiring-velocity conversation with the Head of TA within two weeks.
> **Expected outcome:** Discovery on volume and agency spend; Hiring Assistant pilot scoped.
> **Don't:** Lead with seat counts or pricing.
> **Needs seller's call:** Whether the regional CHRO or local HR head owns the hub's budget.
> **Seller approval required:** Yes.
