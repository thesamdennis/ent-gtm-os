# Risk signal catalogue

Weight these by deal size and stage. A single strong signal can justify Amber; two or more usually mean Amber or Red.

## Strong (each can move status on its own)

- Economic buyer has never been met, at proposal stage or later
- Close date moved twice
- Champion left, changed role, or went silent 21+ days
- Procurement engaged with no agreed decision process or timeline
- Buyer requested a price reduction before confirming value
- Competitor introduced late in the cycle
- Renewal: executive sponsor left; usage down 25%+; open severity-1 escalation

## Moderate

- No next meeting booked
- Mutual action plan steps overdue
- Stage age well above the seller's typical
- Single-threaded (one active contact)
- Legal or security review started late
- New stakeholder appears with unclear role
- Renewal: value story not refreshed in 6+ months; adoption flat; budget-holder change

## Positive signals (note them too)

- Economic buyer attended or initiated a meeting
- Customer shared internal data or an internal deck
- Customer-led next step (they booked it, not us)
- Procurement shared a timeline and template
- Champion introduced a new senior stakeholder

## APAC-specific watchpoints

- Fiscal year differences (April starts common in Singapore government-linked and Japanese-owned firms; check before assuming budget timing)
- Group-level approval required for subsidiaries of conglomerates or government-linked companies
- Silence after a meeting may signal internal disagreement rather than lack of interest; check with your coach privately
