# Hunter fit gate

Run before any signal research in hunter mode. Adjust thresholds to your segment.

## Must pass

- **Size floor:** 1,000+ employees in-region (large enterprise segment). Below this, route to a different segment rather than drop.

## Pass at least two

| Criterion | Pass if |
|---|---|
| Industry | Talent-intensive: financial services, tech, logistics and aviation, healthcare, manufacturing and semiconductors, hospitality, energy, professional services |
| Hiring or reskilling scale | Visible ongoing hiring, or a public workforce or transformation programme |
| Decision access | A decision-maker for talent or L&D sits in-region (not only at a distant global HQ) |
| Budget signal | Growth, funding, profitable results, or a stated people investment |

## Automatic flags (not fails)

- **Recently signed a competitor:** fit, but likely a long-cycle Not yet. Note the contract timing if known.
- **Global HQ buys centrally:** fit, but the door in may be via the global account team. Flag it.
- **Hiring freeze:** fails "hiring scale" but may pass via reskilling. Check before dropping.

## Output

- **Fit:** proceeds to signal scan.
- **Not a fit:** one-line reason, no further research.
