# Play library

The seller's judgment, encoded. Starting set for a talent acquisition and learning solutions seller (Recruiter, Hiring Assistant, Talent Insights, Learning, enterprise hiring programmes). Swap the product column for your own portfolio.

Edit this file after every win, loss or flat play. It's the most valuable file in the repo.

## Farmer plays (existing customers)

| Signal | Likely pressure | Play |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring surge, new market, new site | Recruiter capacity, time-to-fill, agency spend | Recruiter or Hiring Assistant expansion; agency-spend displacement case |
| Hiring freeze, cost programme | Do more with fewer recruiters | Hiring Assistant productivity case; tool consolidation |
| Restructuring, AI transformation | Reskilling, internal mobility | Learning + Talent Insights skills-gap play |
| New CHRO, Head of TA or L&D | New agenda, first 100 days | Exec alignment meeting with a point of view on their agenda |
| M&A, multi-entity reorganisation | Fragmented contracts, duplicated spend | Enterprise consolidation across entities |
| Workforce planning, expansion plans | Talent supply uncertainty | Talent Insights market-mapping play |
| Renewal within 180 days | Value proof, procurement pressure | Value reconfirmation before any pricing conversation |

## Hunter angles (net-new logos)

| Trigger | Entry buyer | Angle (their problem, not your product) |
|---|---|---|
| New hub or market entry | Head of TA, regional HR lead | "Standing up hundreds of hires in a market where you have no employer brand yet" |
| New CHRO | CHRO (first 100 days) | "What peers in your sector are prioritising in year one of a talent agenda" |
| Restructure or AI programme | CHRO, Head of L&D | "Which roles change, and whether to reskill or rehire" |
| Rapid growth, funding round | COO, Head of People | "Hiring speed without the agency bill" |
| Heavy agency usage visible (many agency-posted roles) | Head of TA, procurement | "Cost per hire and control of the candidate experience" |
| Merger | HR integration lead | "Two talent functions, one hiring standard" |
| Skills shortage reported in their sector | Business unit head, CHRO | "Where the talent actually is, and who else is hiring it" |

## APAC notes

- **Government-linked companies:** decisions often route through group HR or a holding entity; procurement processes are formal and longer. Find the group-level owner early.
- **Family-controlled groups:** the family member or long-serving lieutenant often decides, regardless of title. Relationship and introduction route matter more than the pitch.
- **MNC subsidiaries:** check whether the budget sits in-region or at global HQ before investing in local discovery.
- **Hierarchy and face:** a junior "yes" in a meeting is often courtesy, not commitment. Confirm with the senior owner privately.
