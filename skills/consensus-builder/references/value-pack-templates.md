## CHRO or HR brief

- The talent problem in their terms
- What changes for recruiters, managers, employees
- Outcomes: time-to-fill, quality of hire, internal mobility, skills coverage
- Peer evidence (only if real and referenceable)

## IT or security note

- What connects to what; data flows
- Data residency and protection approach (state facts only; flag anything needing vendor confirmation)
- Implementation effort and owners
- Open questions for the vendor's security team

## Procurement summary

- Scope and term
- Commercial structure and what drives price
- Decision timeline and approval steps
- Basis for comparison (what equivalent alternatives would cost)
