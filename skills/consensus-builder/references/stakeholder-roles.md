# Stakeholder roles

| Role | Definition | Typical titles (talent and learning deals) | Red flag if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | Owns the budget; can say yes when others say no | CHRO, CFO, COO, BU head | Always |
| Champion | Has power and access, and sells for you when you're absent | Head of TA, Head of L&D, HR transformation lead | Always |
| Coach | Gives you inside information but lacks power to drive the decision | HR business partner, TA manager | No |
| Technical evaluator | Judges fit, integration and security | HRIS lead, IT architect, InfoSec | For deals with integration or data transfer |
| User lead | Represents day-to-day users | Recruiting manager, L&D programme manager | For adoption-sensitive deals |
| Finance approver | Signs off on spend and business case | Finance BP, CFO office | Above your org's approval threshold |
| Procurement | Runs commercial process and comparison | Procurement manager, vendor management | Always above a material threshold |
| Legal or security | Contract and data-protection review | Legal counsel, DPO | Where personal data is processed |
| Blocker | Has reason or power to stop it | Incumbent's sponsor, a sceptical approver | Not "missing", but must be identified |

## Champion test

A real champion has all three:
1. **Power:** can influence the decision.
2. **Access:** can get the economic buyer in a room.
3. **Personal win:** gains something (credibility, promotion, a problem solved) if this succeeds.

Missing any one → treat as a coach and keep looking.
